# Sources

- **IEEE 802.1Q, VLAN tagging.** `https://standards.ieee.org/standard/802_1Q-2018.html`
  The standard behind the VLAN trunking and access-port behavior described here.
- **NIST SP 800-207, "Zero Trust Architecture."** `https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final`
  The "nothing trusts anything by default" posture the segmentation enforces.
- **Ubiquiti, UniFi Zone-Based Firewall documentation.** `https://help.ui.com/`
  How zones are defined and networks assigned to them on the gateway.
- **Cisco, VLAN and trunk configuration guide.** `https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/lan-switching/vlan/`
  The switch-side VLAN and pruned-trunk configuration in [../../configs/switch-baseline.cfg](../../configs/switch-baseline.cfg).
