# Sources

- **RFC 1918, "Address Allocation for Private Internets."** `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1918`
  The private ranges used throughout the designs.
- **RFC 4632, "Classless Inter-Domain Routing (CIDR)."** `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc4632`
  Prefix-length notation and the block-size arithmetic behind the VLSM allocations.
- **RFC 950 / RFC 1878, subnetting and variable-length subnet masks.** `https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc1878`
  The mask-to-host-count tables the designs rely on.
- **Cisco, "IP Addressing and Subnetting for New Users."** `https://www.cisco.com/c/en/us/support/docs/ip/routing-information-protocol-rip/13788-3.html`
  The worked-example method for reading an address and prefix on sight.
