# Sources

- **Ubiquiti, UniFi Zone-Based Firewall and firewall-policy documentation.** `https://help.ui.com/`
  The zone matrix, policy ordering by index, and firewall-group mechanics used throughout.
- **NIST SP 800-41 Rev. 1, "Guidelines on Firewalls and Firewall Policy."** `https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/41/r1/final`
  Default-deny posture and the principle of allowing only traffic with a stated reason.
- **NIST SP 800-207, "Zero Trust Architecture."** `https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/207/final`
  The least-privilege, per-flow authorization model the seventeen policies implement.
